### CLIM-000233: Regional monsoon onset and break changes
- **Subfield:** Convection and the hydrological cycle
- **Open:** Predict monsoon onset, withdrawal, and multiweek breaks separately from seasonal totals. Attribute changes to circulation, ocean gradients, land heating, aerosols, and intraseasonal variability.
- **Status:** Longstanding; the 2021 monsoon assessment found improved aggregate projections but major regional and process-dependent uncertainty.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Timing governs planting, reservoir reliability, and dry-spell exposure.
- **Key works:** Wang et al. (2021), [“Monsoons Climate Change Assessment”](https://doi.org/10.1175/BAMS-D-19-0335.1).
- **Related:** Crop phenology under stochastic rainfall; optimal reservoir control.
